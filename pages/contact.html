Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to process a donation?

Donations are typically processed within 24-48 hours of submission. You'll receive a confirmation email with your donor certificate.

Can I donate online or must I visit the store?

You can contact us via email or Instagram to arrange a donation pickup. We're flexible with our donors!

How long is my 50% discount valid?

Donor discounts are valid for one year from the date of your donation. Keep your donor certificate for reference.

What happens to the clothes I donate?

Donated clothes are inspected, cleaned, and either sold in our shop or distributed to community members in need.

Can I track my donation?

Yes! You'll receive a donor certificate with a unique ID that you can use to track your donation.
