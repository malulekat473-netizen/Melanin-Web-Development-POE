Donate Clothes

Make a difference in the community and get rewarded

How Donation Works

Donating clothes to Melanin Son is a simple and rewarding process. Not only do you help the environment by promoting sustainable fashion, but you also receive exclusive benefits as a valued donor.

Donation Benefits

  • Receive 50% discount on all purchases in our shop
  • Support sustainable fashion and environmental conservation
  • Help empower the local community
  • Join a movement celebrating black excellence
  • Get a verified donor certificate

Donation Process

  1. Step 1: Prepare Your Donation

    Gather clean, gently used clothing items in good condition. We accept all types of fashion items.

  2. Step 2: Contact Us

    Reach out to us via email or Instagram to arrange a donation time.

  3. Step 3: Provide Identification

    Bring a valid ID for verification purposes. This helps us track donations and issue donor benefits.

  4. Step 4: Drop Off or Arrange Pickup

    Visit our store in Pretoria North, North Park, or arrange a convenient pickup time.

  5. Step 5: Receive Your Benefits

    Get your donor certificate and enjoy 50% discount on your next shop purchase!

What We Accept

  • Gently used clothing items in good condition
  • All types of fashion items (casual, formal, sportswear)
  • Accessories and fashion-related items
  • Items must be clean and ready to wear

What We Don't Accept

  • Damaged or torn clothing
  • Heavily stained items
  • Items with strong odors
  • Worn-out shoes
