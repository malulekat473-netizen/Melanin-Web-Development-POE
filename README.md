# Melanin Son - Sustainable Fashion & Community Empowerment

## Project Overview

**Melanin Son** is a sustainable fashion platform dedicated to black empowerment and community support. The website enables users to donate clothes and receive exclusive discounts, while also providing a shop to browse and purchase quality fashion items.

**Website:** Melanin Son  
**Slogan:** The Son of Soil  
**Founded:** 2024  
**Founder:** Lethabo Sekati  
**Location:** Pretoria North, North Park, South Africa

## Company Information

### Mission
Melanin Son is dedicated to promoting black empowerment through sustainable fashion. We believe in giving back to the community while providing quality clothing at affordable prices.

### Vision
To become a leading sustainable fashion platform that celebrates black culture, promotes community empowerment, and inspires positive change through conscious fashion choices.

### Values
- **Empowerment:** Uplifting and empowering the black community
- **Sustainability:** Environmentally responsible practices
- **Quality:** High-quality fashion items that stand the test of time
- **Community:** Building strong, supportive relationships
- **Integrity:** Transparency and honesty in all business dealings

### About the Founder
**Lethabo Sekati** - Founder and Visionary

Fashion has been in Lethabo's roots from a young age. With a deep passion for style and a commitment to social responsibility, Lethabo envisioned creating a platform that celebrates black empowerment while promoting sustainable fashion practices.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: How do I donate clothes?**  
A: Visit the "Donate Clothes" page or contact us via email (malulekat473@gmail.com) or Instagram (@melanin_son1) to arrange a donation.

**Q: What is the 25% discount for donors?**  
A: Verified donors receive a 25% discount on all purchases in the Melanin Son shop for one year from their donation date.

**Q: What identification do I need to provide?**  
A: You'll need to provide a valid ID (passport, driver's license, etc.) for verification purposes.

**Q: Can I donate online?**  
A: Yes! Contact us to arrange a convenient pickup or drop-off time.

**Q: What happens to the clothes I donate?**  
A: Donated clothes are inspected, cleaned, and either sold in our shop or distributed to community members in need.

**Q: How can I purchase items from the shop?**  
A: Browse our collection on the Shop page or contact us directly for orders and inquiries.
